## Your first inspection in four steps

1. **Set the machine up.** Follow the [unboxing](getting_started/Unboxing.md) and
   [connection guide](getting_started/Connection_guide.md) to assemble and power the system.
2. **Learn the vocabulary.** A quick read of the [terminology](how_to/terminology.md)
   page makes every other guide easier to follow.
3. **Create a REFERENCE.** Capture a known-good board — see
   [Tips](help/Tips.md) for how to get a clean one, or convert an inspected board with
   [UUI to REFERENCE](how_to/UUI_to_REFERENCE.md).
4. **Inspect and report.** Run the [inspection workflow](how_to/Inspection_workflow.md)
   and generate your report.

!!! tip "Not getting the results you expect?"

    Most detection issues come down to two settings: the
    [sensitivity](how_to/Set_sensitivity.md) and the
    [exclusion areas](how_to/Set_exclusion_area.md) of your REFERENCE.
